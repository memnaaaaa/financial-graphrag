GOLDEN SET v2 (data/questions_v2.jsonl)
=======================================

Status: 39 questions, all hand-checked against the filings (gold_source = "hand-checked").

Types: 9 single, 6 relation (2 hops), 12 relation_text (descriptive answer), 5 relation_deep (3-5 hops),
       4 cross_year, 3 cross_company.

 [a] Gold chunks: regex patterns per fact (scripts/build_questions_v2.py) -> every chunk that states a gold fact
 [b] Automatic check: every number and key phrase of the gold answer appears in the gold chunks
 [c] Hand check of every question and answer against the filings -> data/golden_v2_review.md
 [d] fact_chunks: for each gold fact, the chunks that state it (makes the retrieval stage checkable fact by fact)


Main results (39 questions, fixed chunks, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite)
================================================================

  - Best baseline: bge-small + rerank, k = 10: 69% correct, AllFacts 0.64, hallucination 2.6%, missing 28%.
  - DistilBERT is weaker (51% correct at k = 10); reranking helps it more (64%).
  - Nearly every wrong answer is a retrieval failure (11-18 per configuration at k = 10); 1 is a generation error.
  - Main cause: right filing, wrong passage. Wrong fiscal year of the right company is rare (2 facts).
  - Cross-company: 0% correct everywhere (one company swamps the top 10). Cross-year: 0-50%. Deep (3-5 hops): 40%.
  - Single-hop 100% and descriptive 83-92% with bge-small.
  - Full chunk header vs old short header (same ids and bodies): wrong-year chunks in the top k fall from about 41% to 34%,
    recall@10 0.54 to 0.63 (bge-small + rerank); only cross-year and cross-company AllFacts improve.
